About MGM DABHA

Dharmapuri's Heritage of Authentic Flavors & Hospitality

Serving Authentic Taste

Established near Tomato Market in Palacode, Dharmapuri, Tamil Nadu, MGM DABHA (Owned by Dhinesh Kumar) was born from a passion to serve authentic, unadulterated South Indian, North Indian, Chinese and Tandoori delicacies.

Our recipes blend traditional ground spices with modern culinary hygiene standards. Whether you are dining with family or ordering express delivery to your doorstep, we promise delicious food with fast service and affordable prices.
